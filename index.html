FAQ

Good to know before you book.

Still have a question? Give us a call or send an email and we will get back to you.

How do I book an appointment?

Call the studio at 705-728-2200 or email revolvehairstudiobarrie@gmail.com. Let us know what you are hoping for and whether you would like a specific stylist, and we will find you a time.

Do I need a consultation for hair extensions?

Yes. Extensions can add length, volume and even colour, but the right method (wefts, tapes, K-tips, nano beads or clip ins) depends on your daily lifestyle, how often you want to come in for maintenance, and your hair type. A consultation lets us match you with the best option before anything is installed.

Can you fix colour that was done somewhere else?

Colour correction is something we do often, from unwanted warmth to a full change of direction. Book a consultation so your stylist can assess your hair, map out a plan, and be honest about what can be achieved in one session versus over a few visits.

What products do you use and sell?

We work with Redken, K18, Unite, Oligo, Living Proof and Mint, and carry them on our shelf so you can keep your hair healthy at home. Bond building care is part of every lightening service in the studio.

Do you sell gift cards?

We do. Gift cards are available in studio and can be used toward any service or product.
